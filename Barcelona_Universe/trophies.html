European Royalty

Five Stars — Five European Nights

Each of Barcelona's UEFA Champions League victories tells a unique story.

1992 — Wembley

Ronald Koeman's iconic free-kick in extra time defeated Sampdoria 1–0 at Wembley Stadium. Johan Cruyff's Dream Team claimed their first European Cup, completing a historic transformation of the club.

2006 — Paris

Ronaldinho, Samuel Eto'o, and a brilliant Barça side defeated Arsenal 2–1 in the Paris final. The team recovered from going a man down after 18 minutes.

2009 — Rome

Guardiola's first season culminated at the Stadio Olimpico, where Barça defeated Manchester United 2–0. Messi's headed goal is one of the great Champions League moments.

2011 — Wembley

Returning to Wembley, Barça produced what many consider the finest performance in Champions League history, defeating Manchester United 3–1. Messi, Villa, and Pedro scored.

2015 — Berlin

The MSN treble season ended in Berlin, where Barça defeated Juventus 3–1. Neymar's late goal confirmed another treble for Luis Enrique's side.
