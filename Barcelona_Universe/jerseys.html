Current Kits

The official color schemes and shirt identities worn across domestic and international campaigns.

FC Barcelona · Home

Home Kit

2024–25

The iconic garnet and blue vertical stripes return in their classic form. The home kit celebrates the club's traditional identity with the primary sponsor on the front and the club crest on the left chest. Worn at Camp Nou and home fixtures.

FC Barcelona · Away

Away Kit

2024–25

A bold yellow alternate kit contrasting sharply with the home strip. Away kits traditionally provide strong visual differentiation for away fixtures across Spain and Europe. Modern away designs often incorporate subtle pattern elements.

FC Barcelona · Third

Third Kit

2024–25

A deep navy and royal blue design for use when both home and away kits may clash. Third kits often allow designers more creative freedom, resulting in some of the most visually striking strips in the club's history.

Through The Decades

Iconic Historic Strips

Shirts etched forever in football history alongside the golden generations who wore them.

FC Barcelona · Classic

1990s Classic

Dream Team Era

The deep garnet and navy stripes of the early 1990s are forever associated with Johan Cruyff's Dream Team and the club's first European Cup victory at Wembley in 1992. A collector's classic.

FC Barcelona · Centenary

Centenary Kit

1999–2000

To mark the club's centenary in 1999, Barcelona wore a specially designed kit featuring rich colours and embroidered details celebrating 100 years of history. One of the most beloved special edition kits in the club's history.

FC Barcelona · Throwback

Classic Stripes

1970s Throwback

In the 1970s, Johan Cruyff wore the narrow-stripe Barça shirt to iconic effect. The simple, bold pattern represented an era of revolution for both the club and global football.

Design Philosophy

More Than A Shirt

The FC Barcelona home kit is one of the most recognisable garments in sport. The garnet (dark red, known in Catalan as grana) and blue vertical stripes have been the club's signature since the very early years. The exact colours have evolved slightly over the decades — from narrower stripes in the early days to bolder, more defined bands in the modern era.

The home kit represents not just the club's sporting identity but also Catalan cultural pride. Wearing the Barça shirt carries meaning far beyond football.

Kit design has also served as a commercial vehicle. In recent decades, the club's kit deal with Nike has produced numerous striking designs. Each season, a new home, away, and third kit is released, often featuring unique graphic elements, sustainable materials, and innovations in performance fabric technology.
