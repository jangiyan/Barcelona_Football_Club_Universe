Our Story

The Club That Changed Football

Founded on 29 November 1899 by Swiss sportsman Joan Gamper alongside pioneering football enthusiasts from England, Switzerland, and Catalonia, Futbol Club Barcelona blossomed from an intrepid regional team into a worldwide symbol of athletic beauty and cultural liberty.

Barça's enduring sporting philosophy was forged through the genius of Johan Cruyff. First as a maestro on the pitch in the 1970s and subsequently as the visionary head coach of the immortal 'Dream Team' in the early 1990s, Cruyff transformed football into an art form based on proactive possession, spatial awareness, and creative freedom. His foundational work at La Masia established an unbroken tradition of elite homegrown masters, nurturing icons like Pep Guardiola, Xavi Hernández, Andrés Iniesta, and Lionel Messi.

More than a football institution, FC Barcelona is intrinsically bound to Catalan identity. Embracing the motto "Més que un club", Barcelona provided sanctuary for civic pride and democratic ideals during Spain's most challenging historical epochs. Uniquely owned and governed directly by its passionate club members (socios), Barça continues to champion progressive sporting culture across the world.

Club Creed

Més que un club

More than a club — a philosophy, an identity, a way of life.

First uttered by club president Narcís de Carreras in his 1968 induction address, the phrase "Més que un club" embodies Barcelona's deep-seated social engagement, unwavering democratic governance by its socios, and defense of human rights and Catalan identity. For millions of loyal culers around the globe, Barça transcends 90 minutes on a football pitch—it represents unity, artistic pursuit, and collective human dignity.
