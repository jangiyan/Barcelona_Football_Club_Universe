FWD 19
LY

Lamine Yamal

Right Winger

Spain 🇪🇸

Born 16 July 2007
Nationality Spanish
Position Right Winger
Joined Barça 2010 (La Masia)
Club Since Academy

Born in Esparreguera, Catalonia, Lamine Yamal joined Barcelona's La Masia academy at age three. He made his professional debut at just 15 years old in April 2023, becoming one of the youngest players ever to appear for the club's first team.

In the 2023–24 season, he was instrumental in Barcelona's campaign, drawing comparisons to Lionel Messi for his dribbling ability, creativity, and composure. At just 16, he represented Spain at UEFA Euro 2024, scoring a stunning equaliser in the semi-final against France.

He is widely considered the most exciting young talent in world football. His pace, skill, and confidence on the ball — combined with the maturity of a veteran — make him truly exceptional.

FWD 9
RL

Robert Lewandowski

Centre-forward

Poland 🇵🇱

Born 21 August 1988
Nationality Polish
Position Centre-forward
Joined Barça 2022
Previous Club Bayern Munich

One of the most prolific goalscorers in football history, Robert Lewandowski joined FC Barcelona in the summer of 2022 from Bayern Munich. In his debut La Liga season, he scored 23 league goals and won the Pichichi trophy as the division's top scorer.

Known for his intelligent movement, clinical finishing, link-up play, and leadership, Lewandowski brought both goals and experience to a rebuilding Barcelona squad. He has scored over 600 career club goals and is Poland's all-time leading scorer.

His professionalism, fitness, and work rate have made him an important figure both on and off the pitch.

MID 8
PE

Pedri

Central Midfielder

Spain 🇪🇸

Born 25 November 2002
Nationality Spanish
Position Central Midfielder
Joined Barça 2020
Previous Club Las Palmas

Pedro González López, known simply as Pedri, arrived at Barcelona from Las Palmas in the summer of 2020. Despite being just 18 years old, he became an immediate first-team regular under Ronald Koeman and went on to win the Golden Boy award in 2021.

Pedri is widely regarded as one of the finest midfielders of his generation, combining exceptional technical ability, vision, and football intelligence. His ability to control the tempo of a match, find pockets of space, and execute under pressure recalls the great Barça midfielders before him.

He was a key figure for Spain at Euro 2020 and the 2022 World Cup.

FWD 11
RA

Raphinha

Right Winger / Forward

Brazil 🇧🇷

Born 14 December 1996
Nationality Brazilian
Position Right Winger / Forward
Joined Barça 2022
Previous Club Leeds United

Raphael Dias Belloli, known as Raphinha, joined Barcelona from Leeds United in the summer of 2022. A Brazilian international, he is known for his pace, technical skill, and direct dribbling.

He struggled to establish himself consistently in his first season, but the 2024–25 campaign saw him emerge as one of Barça's most important attacking players under manager Hansi Flick.

His versatility — able to play as a winger or in a more central role — combined with his goal contributions and assists make him a crucial part of Barça's attacking structure.
